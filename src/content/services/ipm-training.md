Integrated pest management (IPM) works when frontline staff know what to report before infestations spread. We deliver concise sessions on inspection points, sanitation priorities that support your pest programme, and how to log issues so your contractor responds to facts, not panic.

Modules can be tailored for kitchen sections, housekeeping, stores, or warehousing. The outcome is fewer emergency call-outs, better audit conversations, and alignment between your cleaning chemicals and residual pest treatments where both affect the same surfaces.
