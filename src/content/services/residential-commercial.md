Households need clear scopes (which rooms, drains, and stores are included), preparation notes in English or Kiswahili where helpful, and realistic timelines for pets, children, and busy kitchens. Commercial and hospitality clients need night or off peak slots, trap and trend reporting, SDS and method documentation, and product choices that align with Pest Control Products Board (PCPB) expectations and procurement policies that take POPs rules into account.

Whether you are managing cockroach gel programmes in a restaurant, rodent monitoring in a warehouse, or bed bug work in furnished lets, we combine inspection, targeted application, and monitoring instead of relying on broadcast spraying alone. Facilities teams get the paperwork they need; residents get explanations they can act on before we arrive.

Farming and aggregation clients often need grain store and yard programmes that tie trapping to cleaning rotations and roof integrity, not ad hoc spraying alone. Food processing and beverage plants benefit from contractor coordination, joint visits with engineering or QA when drains, door seals, or pheromone monitoring need adjustment alongside chemistry changes.
