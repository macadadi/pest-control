Nightly fogging alone rarely sustains results and can waste budget. Effective programmes combine removal or treatment of standing water (tyres, blocked drains, plant trays, ponds), larviciding on an agreed schedule, sentinel monitoring where useful, and ultra-low volume (ULV) or other adult control when monitoring or disease risk supports it.

On the coast and lakeside we align guest or resident communication in English and Kiswahili, with clear re-entry and ventilation guidance after space treatments. HOAs and facility managers receive schedules and summaries they can present to treasuries and boards without relying on anecdote.
