Annual contracts suit sites that need regular inspection, rodent bait station servicing, fly monitoring in food areas, mosquito rounds on large compounds, or moth and stored product pest checks in warehouses. Visit frequency and outputs are agreed upfront; public holidays and known operational shutdowns are built into the calendar where relevant.

When counts spike after waste disruptions, weather events, or supply chain changes, reports tie recommendations to evidence. That supports budget decisions and reduces arguments about whether the programme or the environment drove the change.
