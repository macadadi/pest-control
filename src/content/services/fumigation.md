Fumigation is not a single template. Export maize, herbs, or other commodities under tarp or in chambers requires accurate sack counts, headspace, gas curves, and aeration timing that match phytosanitary and shipping windows. Bulk cereal in silos, bag stacks in warehouses, and sealed containers or ship spaces each need different ventilation plans and safety buffers for staff and neighbours. Residential or light commercial void jobs need clear pet and occupant planning, re-entry times, and communication with neighbours where odour may be noticed.

We document treatments with re-entry notices, monitoring where applicable, and photographs or logs that support your forwarder and buyer. Kenyan exporters benefit when humidity and stack condition are checked before gas is applied, gas alone does not fix moisture problems that show up later in transit.
