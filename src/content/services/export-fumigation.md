Phytosanitary and buyer requirements expect gas exposure, venting, and certificates to reflect real stack geometry, moisture, and container numbers. We work with your counts, forwarder instructions, and port or cold store constraints. Pre treatment moisture logs can reduce arguments about mould risk in transit.

Neighbour notification templates, timestamped tarp or sheet photos, and clear aeration completion records support audits and handovers. If sailing windows are tight, we flag conflicts before gas is committed so you can adjust with your shipper.
