Herb and spice exporters face tight phytosanitary and buyer scrutiny. Prepare neighbour notification templates in Kiswahili and English where county askaris or neighbours may query odour. Timestamp and geotag tarp corner photos so sheet uplift or wind damage is documented factually, not debated at 02:00.

Align consolidator sailing windows with aeration time before committing gas. If the vessel cut-off is impossible alongside label venting, resolve the schedule with your forwarder first, demurrage and rejected loads cost more than an honest delay.
