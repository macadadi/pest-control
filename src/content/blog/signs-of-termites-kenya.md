Kenyan extensions often include conduit sleeves through slabs that subterranean termites can follow. During April-May and other swarm periods, check bathrooms, window sills, and expansion joints with a torch for wings, mud tubes, and fine frass, not only when insurance renewal reminds you about the wooden roof.

Repairs that patch concrete without addressing soil contact or voids first are cosmetic. Coordinate any structural pour with whoever owes you a soil treatment certificate before screed and finishes lock access away.

Second-hand furniture from markets can carry drywood risk, inspect joints, laminate lips, and welds before carrying pieces upstairs. When in doubt, book a termite inspection rather than waiting for insurance exclusions to bite.
