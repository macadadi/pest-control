Long rains and holiday bin schedules often coincide, when collection skips a cycle, cockroach harbourage around compactors and grease traps fills quickly. Pouring hot water or approved biofilm treatments down neglected floor drains on a schedule beats discovering frass photos in a group chat at 03:00.

Date-sticky traps along prep lines weekly. If counts double after a long weekend, share the grid with your waste contractor and review cleaning rosters before assuming the insecticide “failed.” Good preparation and source control usually outperform repeated blanket fogging.

Coordinate with your pest contractor before peak season so gel rotations, non-repellent programmes, and inspection slots are booked, not after tenants or auditors escalate the issue.
