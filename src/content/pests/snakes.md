Snakes enter lit compounds when bush clearing or construction pushes them toward shelter. Most calls need calm identification, safe distance, and referral to Kenya Wildlife Service-aligned responders for venomous or protected species. Vertical phone video rarely shows scale counts, horizontal, gently backlit footage helps experts ID faster.

Gabion and mesh fencing, rodent thinning, and shrub management near sleeping areas reduce repeat visits more sustainably than perimeter poisoning alone.
