Pharaoh ants are a major nuisance in Kenyan clinics, kitchens, and high rise cable trays. They bud when stressed by repellent sprays, so treatment targets bait placement on trails and attractant rotation under trained supervision. Outdoor species may enter from balconies with dripping AC trays or from gardens toward sweet spills.

We photograph trail sources where useful, recommend plumbing or proofing fixes, and supply dated bait station photos for hygiene audits that ask for evidence.
