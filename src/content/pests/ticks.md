Dogs and people pick up ticks after walks in Karura style forest strips, lodge lawns, and peri urban grass. Nymphs often attach unnoticed. Woven door mats and fibrous rugs snag life stages that tiled lobbies miss. Control combines vet prescribed pet treatments, timed environmental applications along paths and kennels where labels allow, and mowing or brush reduction to cut down questing habitat.

Broad spraying of ornamental rows kills the non target insects guests photograph; careful border treatments and host management usually meet liability goals more sustainably.
