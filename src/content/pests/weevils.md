Rice weevils, lesser grain borers, and other beetles respond to sanitation, temperature management, and fumigation where appropriate. Jostled silos wake dormant populations; school feeding and relief stores need dated trap rotations even when management changes.

We align gas schedules with real mesh details, weather, and sailing windows so certificates match the cargo treated, not a fictional stack.
