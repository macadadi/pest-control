Bed bugs spread through luggage, laundry, and second hand furniture. In Kenya’s hospitality sector, high guest turnover and outsourced laundry can move infestations between rooms faster than rumour. Cast skins, dark faecal spotting on mattress piping, and live insects on headboards are key signs. Photograph evidence before stripping linen so technicians can confirm life stage remotely where helpful.

Light introductions may clear with thorough preparation and one structured visit; entrenched sites need programmed visits across hatch cycles with cooperation from housekeeping and security for daytime access. We avoid blaming residents while still asking for full and accurate prep so heat or chemical programmes can work.
