Rodents enter through gaps under roller shutters, broken vents, roof lines, and utility penetrations. In Kenyan logistics and market-adjacent sites, Norway rats often burrow beside waste while roof rats use overhead routes. Snap traps alone beside busy produce bays rarely suffice; integrated programmes use proofing schedules, secured bait stations, tracking dust judiciously where legal, and dated service records.

Warehouses preparing for ISO or customer audits benefit from photo logs of station checks and repairs. We tie recommendations to harbourage (pallets, weeds, drainage), not only to poison refills.
