Honeybee swarms on school roofs, fences, or water towers should usually be relocated for pollination value and safety. We identify whether the cluster is a manageable swarm or an established colony that needs a cut out or specialist access, then coordinate with beekeepers and county agriculture desks where response times vary.

Horizontal photos showing comb shape help beekeepers plan. Calling early on a weekday beats discovering the duty officer travelled upcountry at midday.
