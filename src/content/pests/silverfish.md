Silverfish thrive in warm, humid voids, lift motor rooms, file stores, and bathrooms with slow leaks. They damage glue in books, wallpaper, and starch-sized textiles. Desiccant sachets, dehumidification, and rotation of seasonal stock outperform blanket fogging that can harm inks and finishes.

We combine environmental fixes with targeted crack treatments where monitoring shows activity.
