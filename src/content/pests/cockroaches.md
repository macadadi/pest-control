German cockroaches dominate many Kenyan kitchens, hospitals, and food outlets; large peridomestic species often arrive with produce or enter from drains and yards. Broadcast supermarket sprays rarely resolve heavy infestations and can contaminate food surfaces. Professional programmes combine non repellent baits, crack and crevice treatment, growth regulators where appropriate, and sanitation coaching.

Sticky monitors show whether counts are rising after bin delays or cleaning gaps, useful for KEBS minded sites and for proving progress to landlords. SDS binders should match products in use; we align applications with your audit and HACCP context.
