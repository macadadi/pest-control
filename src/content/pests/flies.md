House flies, fruit flies, and biting stable flies each need different tactics. Improve cleaning of drains and waste areas, manage rot material promptly, and service UV insect light traps on a schedule. Packers exporting fresh produce often keep rolling trap logs that match EU style questionnaires.

We align contractor visits with your worst pressure points (markets, landing sites, grease traps) instead of spraying guest areas without dealing with the source.
