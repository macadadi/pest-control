Paper wasps and hornets favour sheltered beams, plastic wrapped dock areas, and rooflines. Kenyan HSE and site rules often need night or low traffic slots, taped off bays, and communication to security before dust or foam application. We mark re-entry and remove nests when safe so loaders and cleaners are not caught out.

Early intervention avoids medical incidents and production stops during peak dispatch.
