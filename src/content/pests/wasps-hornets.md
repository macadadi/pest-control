Paper wasps and hornets favour sheltered beams, plastic-wrapped dock areas, and rooflines. Kenyan HSE and site rules often require night or low-traffic slots, taped-off bays, and communication to security before dust or foam application. We mark re-entry and remove nests when safe so loaders and cleaners are not surprised.

Early intervention avoids medical incidents and production stops during peak dispatch.
