Aedes mosquitoes breed in small, clean water containers (from plant saucers to blocked gutters) while other species use larger ponds and poorly drained fields. Nightly fogging may calm complaints in the short term but wastes budget if breeding sites remain. We GPS or map pools, schedule larvicide where appropriate, clear or treat tyres and drains with owner consent, and apply adult control when thresholds or public health context justify it.

Kenyan county health teams increasingly ask what was treated; clear records help hotels, schools, and HOAs answer the question without guesswork.
