Indianmeal moths infest grain, flour, and dried goods; case making and webbing clothes moths attack wool and natural fibres near heaters or poorly ventilated wardrobes. Triangle traps at chest height catch supervisor attention in stores and pack houses. County aggregation yards mix old sack dust, so trap placement and stock rotation matter as much as fumigation timing.
