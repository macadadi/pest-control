Kenyan compounds often mix poultry, dogs, bare soil, and tiled interiors, fleas move between hosts and environment freely. Vacuuming and washing bedding help, but larvae persist in grout and carpet edges. Professional treatment targets floors and voids with approved growth regulators and residuals while pets receive on-animal products from your vet.

Doing only the house or only the pet usually brings the problem back within weeks. We coordinate timing and re-entry so the programme sticks.
