Huntsman and other large spiders alarm guests after storms when they move across bathroom tiles or ceilings. Most are beneficial hunters of midges and flies. Control emphasises vacuuming webs, sealing loft and curtain-track gaps, and exterior lighting adjustments that reduce prey attraction, resorting to insecticides only where labels, species risk, or client standards require.

Coastal humidity increases cobweb buildup; scheduled dewebbing plus proofing often resolves complaints without fogging delicate interiors.
