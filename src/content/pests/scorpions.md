Bark scorpions and related species use stacked pavers, firewood piles, irrigation valve boxes, and cladding gaps. Perimeter glue monitoring, leak repair, and tidy firewood storage reduce repeat encounters more reliably than random pesticide strips across lawns. Thin sandals on dewy grass invite incidents; we advise practical footwear rules for staff and families.

FM humidity logs beside expansion joints help explain repeat encounters to insurers that increasingly expect dated photo annexes.
