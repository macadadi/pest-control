Coastal properties face higher night-time mosquito activity, ant trails toward air-conditioning condensate, cockroach pressure in warm service areas, and occasional scorpion encounters near stacked materials and irrigation. Loading bays and lit docks can also harbour wasps and stinging insects. Hotels, cafés, and port-adjacent stores need programmes that protect reputation as well as structure, guest-facing areas, back-of-house, and dry stores are scoped separately.

Treatments respect label performance in humid, salty air and include clear English and Kiswahili communication for fogging or ULV schedules so guests and tenants understand re-entry and ventilation. Resorts, HOAs, and facility managers get schedules and logs they can defend to boards and county health enquiries.
