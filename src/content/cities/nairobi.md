Nairobi combines high-rise apartments, sprawling estates, logistics hubs, and food retail with intense pest pressure when waste collection slips or weather spikes fly and cockroach numbers. Clinics and hospitals often battle Pharaoh ants in warm cable voids; warehouses near markets contend with roof rats and entry at loading bays. Mixed-use corridors from the CBD through Westlands and out toward industrial edges see different night-time fly and rodent patterns than quiet suburban courts.

We plan access with estate security, hospital protocols, and traffic realities for day or night work, including sites in Kiambu and Machakos where compound rules and longer commutes affect how crews stage equipment. Programmes blend inspection, targeted treatment, and monitoring so residential clients get clear prep and re-entry guidance while commercial clients receive documentation suitable for procurement and audits.
