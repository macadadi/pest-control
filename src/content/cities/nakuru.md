Nakuru and surrounding highland areas host export-oriented pack houses, cold stores, and logistics corridors where stable flies, rodents, and stored-product pests must be managed to audit standards. Dairy, floriculture, and peri-urban smallholdings add their own fly, ant, and rodent pressure where feed and waste lines meet roads. Residential and lodge clients contend with termites in soil-contact details, silverfish in humid storerooms, and seasonal shifts that affect spray films and bait palatability.

We align programmes with night temperatures, UV exposure on external treatments, and loading-bay hygiene so monitoring tells a clear story to agronomists, QA teams, and household clients alike.
