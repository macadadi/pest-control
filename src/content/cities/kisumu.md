Kisumu’s lakeside climate supports strong mosquito breeding in grass swales, tyres, and blocked drains, while fish and produce handling drives fly counts that spike when waste or market logistics stall. Grain yards and stores need weevil and stored-product pest programmes tied to stock rotation and trap trends.

We coordinate with community and county expectations where larvicide or large-site work is sensitive, and we document fly and rodent monitoring so improvements (or sanitation incidents) show clearly in the data. Schools, markets, and public buildings benefit from scheduled inspections and bilingual notices after space treatments so administrators can brief parents and vendors without guesswork.
